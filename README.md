This is a GUI based project that works like a online Shopping platform where you first have to create an account.
After successful creation of your account you can purchase different items from the store and later you have to pay the amount via your online shopping account,bank account or by cash on delivery.
This project during sign in collects all the essential data that is needed to create a account(i.e:Name, Password, Phone No, Email and Address) and later uses that information during a purchase by a customer.
The Atroves also have a admin panel option where the admin(owner) can manage the stocks(i.e:Add or remove items) and can also see the numbers of orders placed and the numbers of users and can also edit users information(i.e:delete user, update user account balance, update user information).
Thus, after successful purchase it generates a receipt that shows all the items brought and their prices and total price calculated at the end.
It also have a option of paying via bank card where certain discounts are given based upon card type.
